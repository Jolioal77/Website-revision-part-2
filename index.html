Pizza

Pizza is a popular food that features a round, flat base made of baked dough. This delicious base is typically covered in rich tomato sauce and melted cheese. People often add extra toppings like pepperoni, mushrooms, and fresh vegetables. It is usually cut into neat slices so it is easy to share with friends and family. Many people enjoy eating pizza at parties, gatherings, or cozy nights at home.

Pizza is one of the world's most popular foods, and its history is filled with surprising facts. While many people associate pizza entirely with Italy, modern researchers believe its earliest ancestors were flatbreads with toppings eaten by ancient Greeks and Egyptians. The world's most popular pizza topping is pepperoni, which features on roughly 36% of all pies ordered. If you love pizza, you might want to visit São Paulo, Brazil, which holds the title of the pizza capital of the world with over 11,000 pizzerias serving its citizens. Finally, pizza has even traveled beyond Earth; in 2001, a popular fast-food chain successfully delivered a six-inch pizza to astronauts on the International Space Station.
